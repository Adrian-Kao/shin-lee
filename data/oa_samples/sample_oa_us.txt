Claims 1-3 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. § 103 as being obvious over US7654321
in view of US6543210.

The cited references, when combined, render the claimed microchannel cooling
system with non-uniform cross-section obvious. US7654321 discloses microchannels
with width modulation, and US6543210 teaches the use of copper as a thermal
conductor. The combination yields predictable results.

Claims 4-5 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. § 102 as anticipated by US7654321.
The reference discloses every limitation of these claims.
