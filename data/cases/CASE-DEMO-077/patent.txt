【名稱】Solid-state LiDAR with MEMS Mirror

【摘要】
A solid-state LiDAR module employing a MEMS mirror, a 905 nm laser diode, and a SPAD receiver array.

【說明書節錄】
Background: Mechanical LiDAR is bulky.
Description: Compact MEMS-based solid-state LiDAR.


【申請專利範圍】
請求項 1：1. A LiDAR module comprising: a 905 nm laser diode; a MEMS mirror configured to scan the laser beam; and a single-photon avalanche diode (SPAD) array configured to receive reflected photons.
請求項 2：2. The module of claim 1, wherein the MEMS mirror has a scan angle of at least 60 degrees.
