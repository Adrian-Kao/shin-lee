【名稱】Dynamic Load Balancing for EV Charging Stations

【摘要】
A method for dynamically balancing load across a cluster of EV charging stations based on real-time grid frequency and state-of-charge data.

【說明書節錄】
Background: Static load allocation under-utilizes capacity.
Description: Dynamic balancing improves throughput and grid stability.


【申請專利範圍】
請求項 1：1. A method for load balancing comprising: receiving grid frequency measurements; receiving SOC data from a plurality of EV charging sessions; computing per-station current limits based on frequency deviation and SOC priority; and transmitting the limits to each station.
請求項 2：2. The method of claim 1, further comprising prioritizing low-SOC sessions during peak hours.
請求項 3：3. The method of claim 1, wherein the limits are recomputed every 100 milliseconds.
