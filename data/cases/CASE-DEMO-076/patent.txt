【名稱】Federated Learning with Differential Privacy

【摘要】
A federated learning system that aggregates local model updates with calibrated Gaussian noise injection for differential privacy.

【說明書節錄】
Background: Standard FedAvg leaks information.
Description: DP-FedAvg with calibrated noise.


【申請專利範圍】
請求項 1：1. A federated learning method comprising: receiving local gradient updates from a plurality of clients; adding Gaussian noise to the updates; and aggregating the noisy updates into a global model.
請求項 2：2. The method of claim 1, wherein the Gaussian noise variance is calibrated to achieve a target epsilon.
請求項 3：3. The method of claim 1, wherein clipping is applied to gradients before noise injection.
