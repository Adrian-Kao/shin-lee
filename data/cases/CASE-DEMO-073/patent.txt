【名稱】GaN HEMT Power Transistor Structure

【摘要】
A gallium nitride high-electron-mobility transistor (GaN HEMT) structure with an integrated field plate to reduce peak electric field.

【說明書節錄】
Technical field: GaN power transistor.
Background: Conventional GaN HEMT suffers from peak field at gate edge.
Description: Field plate redistributes E-field, increasing breakdown voltage.


【申請專利範圍】
請求項 1：1. A GaN HEMT comprising: a substrate; a GaN buffer layer disposed on the substrate; an AlGaN barrier layer disposed on the buffer layer; a gate electrode disposed on the barrier layer; and a field plate connected to the gate electrode and extending toward the drain.
請求項 2：2. The HEMT of claim 1, wherein the field plate length is between 0.5 and 2 micrometers.
請求項 3：3. The HEMT of claim 1, wherein the AlGaN barrier layer thickness is between 15 and 25 nanometers.
