EUROPEAN PATENT OFFICE
Communication pursuant to Article 94(3) EPC

Application No.: 19 123 456.7
Applicant: NCCU Apex Patent Law Firm (file ref: APEX-2025-EP07, client: CL-EVCO12)
Examining Division: Munich
Date of this communication: 2025-04-15

The examination of the above-identified application has revealed that it does
not meet the requirements of the European Patent Convention for the reasons set
out below. The applicant is invited to file observations and, where appropriate,
amendments within FOUR MONTHS of the date of this communication.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. Inventive step - Article 56 EPC
------------------------------------------------------------
Claims 1-3 do not involve an inventive step within the meaning of Art. 56 EPC.
Document D1 (EP3210987) discloses a wireless charging coil arrangement, and the
distinguishing features of claim 1 amount to an obvious workshop modification
that the skilled person would apply when faced with the objective technical
problem. The combination of D1 with the common general knowledge evidenced by D2
renders the claimed subject-matter obvious.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. Novelty - Article 54 EPC
------------------------------------------------------------
Claim 4 lacks novelty under Art. 54 EPC. All of its features are directly and
unambiguously derivable from D1 (EP3210987).

------------------------------------------------------------
3. Clarity - Article 84 EPC
------------------------------------------------------------
Claim 5 does not meet the requirements of Art. 84 EPC: the expression
"substantially aligned" is unclear and is not supported by the description.

The applicant's attention is drawn to Art. 123(2) EPC: any amendment must not
introduce subject-matter extending beyond the content of the application as
filed.
