【名稱】Wearable Continuous Glucose Monitor with Adhesive Patch

【摘要】
A wearable continuous glucose monitor employing a subcutaneous sensor coupled to an adhesive patch and a wireless transmitter.

【說明書節錄】
Background: Existing CGMs use fingerstick calibration.
Description: Hydrocolloid adhesive improves wear time; BLE transmitter reduces power.


【申請專利範圍】
請求項 1：1. A continuous glucose monitor comprising: a subcutaneous enzyme-based glucose sensor; an adhesive patch supporting the sensor; and a wireless Bluetooth Low Energy transmitter coupled to the sensor.
請求項 2：2. The monitor of claim 1, wherein the adhesive patch comprises a hydrocolloid layer.
請求項 3：3. The monitor of claim 1, wherein the transmitter has a battery life of at least 14 days.
